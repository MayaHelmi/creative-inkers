Seller questions

Can I use these on Etsy?
Yes. Printing a design on a physical product and selling it on Etsy is exactly what the commercial licence covers. Keep your licence PDF — Etsy sometimes asks sellers to show rights to artwork.
What about Amazon Merch?
Yes, POD platforms where you upload art and they print physical items are covered. What’s not covered is any platform that resells the file itself.
How are unit limits tracked?
On trust — we don’t audit your sales. If you pass 500 units on a design, upgrade that design to the extended licence. Most sellers never get close.
Can I sell at craft fairs and market stalls?
Yes. In-person sales count the same as online sales — finished physical items are always fine.
Can I give the file to my printer?
Yes. Handing the file to a print shop that prints on your behalf is part of normal use. They can’t keep it, reuse it for other clients, or sell it.
What happens to my rights if I cancel my membership?
Nothing. Every file you downloaded while a member keeps its licence forever. Cancelling stops new downloads, never old rights.
Do the designs ever get taken down?
If a design leaves the library, existing buyers keep the file and the licence. Your purchases are never revoked.
What’s the refund policy?
14 days if a file won’t print as described — wrong resolution, corrupt file, missing transparency. Because it’s a digital file, “changed my mind” after download isn’t refundable.
