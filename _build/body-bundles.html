Questions

What happens if I cancel?
You keep every file you downloaded while you were a member, with its commercial licence intact. You stop getting new drops and library access on the day your billing period ends. Nothing is taken back.
Can I sell what I print?
Yes. Every purchase — single, vault or membership — includes the commercial licence. You can print the design on physical products and sell them in your shop, at markets, or on POD marketplaces. What you can’t do is resell the file itself.
How many items can I sell?
Up to 500 physical items per design on the standard commercial licence. Past that, the extended licence covers unlimited units — see the licence page for the tier table.
Do you ever take designs down?
If a design leaves the library, everyone who downloaded it keeps it and keeps the licence. Your library is permanent.
What formats do I get?
Every design ships as a 300 DPI transparent PNG plus an SVG vector source. Most also include a 600 DPI PNG for large-format printing.
Do you offer refunds?
14 days, if the file won’t print as described — wrong resolution, damaged file, missing transparency. Because the product is a digital file, we can’t refund “changed my mind” once it’s downloaded.
